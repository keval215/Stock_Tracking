# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
A single person (the owner) reviewing the Indian equity market after the NSE close, mostly in the evening. They keep watchlists and a portfolio, scan the whole market for setups, and set price and scanner alerts for the next session. Personal tool: no team, no onboarding funnel.

## Product Purpose
NSE Pulse turns the daily NSE end-of-day BhavCopy into a personal decision desk: whole-market scanners and a custom scanner builder, sector performance and category heatmaps, watchlists, portfolio profit and loss, after-market alerts, and an MCP server so AI clients can query the same data. Success is finishing the evening review quickly and knowing what changed.

## Positioning
Whole-NSE end-of-day scanning, with the user's own watchlists, portfolio and alerts in the same place, plus an MCP interface to the data.

## Operating Context
Data is end-of-day only (no live ticks). Prices in INR, Indian number formatting. Light and dark themes; dark is used for evening reading.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Watchlist, Portfolio, Market heatmap, Scanners, Scanner builder, Alerts, MCP server, global stock lookup, stock detail drawer with candlestick and trend charts and indicators. Functionality must not change in a visual redesign. React 19, Tailwind 4, Recharts and D3.

## Brand Commitments
Name: NSE Pulse. No existing logo or brand colour.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, customer counts or benchmarks exist; none may be added.

## Product Principles
- The tool disappears into the task: scanability and consistency over expression.
- Figures are the content: tabular, aligned, one format.
- Colour carries meaning (gain, loss, selection), not decoration.
- Quiet chrome; richness only in the heatmap and charts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keep WCAG AA contrast in both themes, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support.
